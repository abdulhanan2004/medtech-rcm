Medical Billing

Practical billing support from charge capture through claim submission, payment posting and issue resolution.

Cleaner billing workflow

Medical billing requires consistent front-end information, accurate claim preparation, timely submission and disciplined follow-up.

What We Can Handle

✓ Patient and insurance information review
✓ Charge entry and claim creation
✓ Claim scrubbing and payer edits
✓ Electronic claim submission
✓ Rejection review and correction
✓ Payment posting workflow
✓ EOB/ERA review
✓ Billing and performance reporting

How the Workflow Works

We begin by understanding the practice's current process, payer mix, specialty requirements and operational priorities. The workflow is then documented so responsibilities, follow-up actions and reporting expectations are clear.

Throughout the cycle, issues are tracked at the appropriate level — patient, claim, payer, denial or A/R bucket — so the practice can focus on decisions instead of chasing disconnected billing tasks.

Reporting & Visibility

Depending on the practice's systems and available data, reporting can include aging trends, claim status, denial categories, payment activity, follow-up actions and other operational indicators.

Who This Can Help

Independent practices, specialty groups and healthcare organizations looking for additional billing workflow capacity or a more structured revenue-cycle process can use this service as part of a broader RCM engagement.
